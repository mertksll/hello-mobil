<h1>Terms of use</h1>
<h2>Scope of use</h2>
<p>Kampus's Coffee lets you customize coffee choices and manage order records. This version stores records on your device; it does not transmit orders to cafés or process payments.</p>

<p>Do not enter real payment details or sensitive personal data. Check your classroom details. You can delete local records from the profile page. No backup is provided.</p>
