<article className="info-page">
  <h1>Ordering guide</h1>
  <p>Choose your coffee, set your preferences and keep track of your order records in one place.</p>
  <section>
    <h2>1. Choose your coffee</h2>
    <p>Select a café or search for a coffee on Discover. Open the product details to choose size, milk and quantity, then add it to your cart.</p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <h2>2. Review your cart</h2>
    <p>Each cart belongs to one café. If you try to add an item from another café, your current cart stays intact and an explanation appears. Five or more coffees from the same café for the same classroom receive a calculated 10% group discount.</p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <h2>3. Add classroom details</h2>
    <p>Check the building, floor and classroom. Classroom preferences saved in your profile are filled in automatically for your next cart.</p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <h2>4. View your record</h2>
    <p>After confirmation, find the tracking code and summary in My orders. Reorder adds the items to your cart while keeping the single-café rule.</p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <h2>About confirmation</h2>
    <p>In this version, confirmation only creates an order record on your device. No payment is taken and no order is sent to a business. See the terms for details.</p>
  </section>
