<h1>Privacy</h1>
<h2>Information stored on your device</h2>
<p>The theme preference (tema), cart (kampus-cart) and demo order history (kampus-orders) are stored in this browser’s localStorage. Orders include coffee options, a sample classroom address, amount, code and date. Records do not expire automatically.</p>
<h2>Form and links</h2>
<p>The contact form only displays a demo notice and clears its fields; it does not send or store their contents. The app adds no analytics or tracking cookies. External GitHub links follow that site’s terms. A web hosting provider may keep its own access logs.</p>
<h2>Deletion and security</h2>
<p>The profile delete button removes the cart and order history. Clear the browser’s site data to remove all records including the theme. localStorage is not an encrypted vault; other people using the device may access it. Do not enter real or sensitive personal information.</p>
