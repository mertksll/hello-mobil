<h1>About</h1>
<h2>Bringing your coffee break to class</h2>
<p>Kampus's Coffee began with a simple problem: waiting in a café queue between lessons. Students choose coffee from one café, customize it and group orders for the same classroom. This educational demo does not process real payments or deliveries.</p>
<h2>Developer</h2>
<p>Mustafa Mert Köksal · 2620511152 · Information Security Technology · Istinye University Vocational School. MYO063 Mobile Programming; instructor Keyvan Arasteh Abbasabad.</p>
<h2>Technologies</h2>
<p>Tauri v2 and Rust: native application layer; Astro: static pages; Svelte 5: interactive interface; React: the counter below; MDX: information pages; Bun: dependencies and builds.</p>
<h2>Demo scope</h2>
<p>Starbucks, Kahve Dünyası and Espressolab are sample café choices. There is no partnership; prices and discounts are fictional examples. Real integrations, accounts and courier management are future work.</p>
